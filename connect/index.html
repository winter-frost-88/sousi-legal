SousiYour kitchen, connected

Your recipes and receipts, in ChatGPT.

Find something to cook, compare your saved recipes, or plan a meal for a crowd—with your Sousi library and uploaded receipts at hand.

Connect Sousi

  1. In ChatGPT’s plugin settings, add a custom MCP server.
  2. Use this server address and choose OAuth authentication.
  3. Return here to sign in and approve access when ChatGPT opens this page.

Custom connections depend on your ChatGPT account and workspace settings. ChatGPT handles the conversation; Sousi provides your saved, synced recipes and receipts.

Sign in to Sousi

Use the same email and password you use in the Sousi app.

Need to reset your password? Open Account in the Sousi app. This page does not create a new account.

Allow read-only access?

  • Search saved recipes in your current Sousi household.
  • Read ingredients, instructions, servings, timings and source links.
  • Browse your cookbooks.
  • Search uploaded receipts in your current Sousi household.
  • Read stores, dates, totals, and recognized items with prices and quantities.

It cannot change your data, read shopping lists, or make purchases. Information returned to ChatGPT becomes part of your conversation there.

Your connections

You can disconnect access here at any time. Disconnecting stops future access; it does not remove information already shared in a ChatGPT conversation.
